QUICK ANSWERS

Frequently asked
questions.

Everything you need to choose the simplest integration for your site.

Do I need to install a JavaScript library?+

No. A regular iframe is enough for a complete player. Use the SDK only when you want programmatic mounting, deeper UI integration, or direct access to player methods.

Can I use my own colors, audio, and subtitles?+

Yes. URL parameters control the accent, start time, preferred audio and subtitle languages, playback behavior, controls, and more. The player also supports separately hosted audio tracks and VTT, SRT, or ASS subtitle files.

What happens when a source fails or lacks a language?+

The player checks compatible sources instead of declaring a language unavailable after one failure. It ranks healthy multi-audio sources first, races sources by real latency, and can fail over to standby streams or a full backend relay.

Can I connect playback to my analytics?+

Yes. The origin-locked PostMessage bridge emits playback, engagement, quality, language, buffering, and recovery events. Send those signals to any analytics pipeline you already use.

Does it work on mobile and with TV series?+

The controls are responsive and touch-friendly. TV and anime embeds include season/episode metadata, episode selection, next-episode prewarming, intro/outro skipping, and optional auto-next playback.
